About Paws & Home

Founded in 2018, Paws & Home is a no-kill shelter dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need. Every animal in our care receives veterinary attention, socialization, and lots of love while waiting for their forever family.

We've placed over 2,500 pets in loving homes and counting. Whether you're looking for a playful puppy or a mellow senior cat, we'll help you find the perfect match.
