Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our adoption application form, we collect the personal information you provide, including your full name, email address, phone number, and details about your living situation. We do not collect information automatically through cookies or tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to process adoption applications and communicate with you about available pets. We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored securely and access is limited to authorized shelter staff. We retain application data for up to 12 months after submission, after which it is permanently deleted.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To make a request, please contact us using the information on our application page.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our contact form or email us at privacy@pawsandhome.org.
